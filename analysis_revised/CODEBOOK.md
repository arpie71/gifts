# Codebook: revised analysis datasets

Generated by `analysis_revised/05_codebook.py` from the files in `analysis_revised/data/`. Summary statistics are computed from the current build.

Conventions: *prior* and *lagged* measures use only information dated before year t. Career information comes from Wikidata (snapshot `files/wikidata_claims.csv` plus `analysis_revised/cache/claims_supplement.csv`); it is missing for unlinked people and incomplete for junior staff, and missingness is not random (see `tab_audit_by_group`).
